# News Desk — Canonical Editorial Product Rules v1.0
Date: 2026-10-11
Decision owner: DrivMatch News
Status: News Desk naming APPROVED; reader UI changes implemented subject to build/deploy; original evidence-backed article generator still separate future work.

## Naming
The editorial system is **News Desk**, never Dispatch Desk. The existing dispatcher profession remains labeled Dispatcher. Historical Dispatch Desk project documents are legacy references, not the current editorial product name.

## Canonical reader experience
- One lead article in the carousel at a time.
- Distinct adjacent "Leia Mais / Read More" companion stories.
- Older coverage remains visible below as an archive/news list and does not duplicate the simultaneously featured or adjacent stories.
- Deduplicate canonical events by source URL, normalized title and event grouping whenever reliable; do not delete historical valid coverage solely because a related updated story exists.
- Avoid promotional repeated headlines and preserve genuine updates as explicitly dated developments.

## Inside article
Exactly two separate professional analysis actions, **Motorista** and **Dispatcher**, opening a popup/dialog resembling the existing share-selector experience (not inline article text). Each popup contextualizes the same story for its profession, distinguishes verified facts from possible operational impact, and offers practical points to check. Keep third-party original-source attribution and the complete article/brief experience intact.

## Sharing
Retain existing share destinations and controls: WhatsApp, Facebook, Threads, X, LinkedIn, Telegram, Reddit, Pinterest, email, story card, device share when available. **Do not author a special WhatsApp article.** Shared link previews should display the news title plus a legally usable 1200×630 DrivMatch-branded social image. Existing share page generator creates Open Graph og:title/og:image and Twitter summary_large_image; preserve this functionality and its licensed/illustrative image fallback. Validate social card URLs after deploy.

## Truthfulness and readiness
External RSS discovery links are not automatically complete original articles. The detailed two-role impact compiler remains a separate editorial development gate; category-specific checkpoints must be labeled as such rather than fabricated fact-specific consequences.
