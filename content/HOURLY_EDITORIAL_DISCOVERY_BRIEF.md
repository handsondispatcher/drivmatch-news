# DrivMatch News — PERMANENT HOURLY EDITORIAL DISCOVERY BRIEF v1.1

## Cadence
Run discovery automatically at least once each UTC hour, 24/7 including Saturdays, Sundays and US holidays. Existing 10-minute deployment schedule remains for other news/weather/video functions. Never depend on a person asking for updates. Do not claim a new publication merely because a scan completed.

## Audience
US truck drivers (CDL and non-CDL), dispatchers, carriers, fleet owners, freight brokers, shippers, owner-operators, hotshot operators, box truck, cargo van, towing, car hauler and trailer operators.

## Source universe — continually expand and validate
National and regional US TV stations, local TV news and their websites; print and online newspapers; specialist trucking/logistics and trade magazines; all relevant federal/state/county/municipal government and DOT/511/Highway Patrol/FAA/NHTSA/FMCSA/EIA/BLS/CBP/port bodies; US universities and research centers; carriers, fleets, truck brokers, shippers, truck stops and fuel networks; OEMs, commercial vehicles, trailers, equipment and component makers; trucking insurers and service vendors; freight marketplaces; technology and telematics; M&A and management publications; public LinkedIn and X reports as discovery signals needing independent validation. No claim that ALL sites are continuously monitored without authorized APIs/working feeds; maintain per-source monitorable coverage and failures.

## Mandatory search territories
1. Truck accidents, fatal/major incidents, hazmat, crashes, closures, restrictions, lane blockages, detours, roadworks, weather-related trucking impacts, bridge/tunnel/port/border disruptions, highway police and DOT alerts.
2. Freight rates, loads, capacity, dispatch, brokers, carrier profitability, contracts, fraud, double-brokering, cargo theft, factoring, insurance, industry regulation, CDL and work rules.
3. Freight modes and equipment: general freight, dry van, reefer/refrigerated/frozen, container drayage, intermodal, flatbed, step deck, Conestoga, sider/curtainsider, car hauling, towing, hotshot, box truck, cargo van, semi trucks, trailers.
4. Launches and recalls: trucks, tractors, vans, reefer units, trailers, ELD, TMS, dispatch systems, autonomous trucks, AI, maintenance, fuel innovations.
5. US fuel prices, diesel, truck stop chains, repair and roadside services, truck parking, rest areas, wash stations, driver services, broker/dispatcher tools.
6. Earnings, bankruptcies, mergers and acquisitions, fleet management, logistics technology, university research and market forecasts.

## Evidence and editorial safety
Discover widely; publish selectively. Each published source-linked item MUST have a verifiable original publisher URL, original publication timestamp, explicit credit, verifiable US trucking relevance and reasonably reliable source; government/official alerts are labelled as official source notices, not claimed as confirmed road closures without road authority corroboration. Google News is discovery only, never credited as original publisher. LinkedIn/X are leads, not independently verified facts. Do not rewrite or reproduce copyrighted full articles; publish original DrivMatch context only when independently sourced and properly attributed. No synthetic reports, fabricated quotations, invented incidents or inaccurate live status. Translate headlines into the reader's language only when a real validated translation exists. Preserve each source's original title, link and timestamp. De-duplicate across publisher headlines and homepage surfaces by canonical URL and event identity.

## Editorial organization
A story has one primary homepage placement. Hero, related rail, top headlines and feed must not recycle it. Rank by operational urgency, freshness, geographic scope and diversity across transportation verticals. Some Sundays may have few genuinely new verified stories: never pad with old stories or fake news. Clearly differentiate newly verified breaking alerts, fresh reports and evergreen analysis.

## Reliability / escalation
Track every discovery run: timestamp, monitored source count, working feed count, discovered candidates, eligible publisher links, published articles, source failures, newest original publication timestamp and gap-hours since latest verified article. If no fresh relevant article appears within 3 hours, broaden active priority feeds and create an operational incident; do not fabricate. If RSS fails, retain correctly dated verified material within declared TTL and investigate feed adapters. Periodically review manual-review sources; move to automated only after verifying an authorized stable API/RSS/Atom endpoint. Operators are not required to manage routine updates.
